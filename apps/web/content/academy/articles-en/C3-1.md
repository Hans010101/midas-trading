# Moving Average Strategy

> Midas Academy · C3 Strategy Topic Library

Moving Average strategy is the oldest and most well-known trend strategy, and it is almost every trader's first lesson. This article follows the structure of "Principle → Applicability/Failure → Usage → Advantages, Disadvantages and Risks → How to try it in Midas" to explain it thoroughly - the focus is not on "whether you can make money by using it", but on its logic and applicable boundaries**.

**Quick overview of this article**

- Moving Average strategy = **Use the Moving Average direction/cross/arrangement to determine the trend and follow the trend**; do not predict the top or bottom, but follow the established trend.
- **Applicable to unilateral trends, invalid in shocks** (Golden CrossDeath Cross has many false signals in shocks).

## 1. Strategy principles

Moving Average (MA) is the average of the closing prices of the last N periods, rolling into a line (see B1). It "smooths out" short-term fluctuations in price, allowing you to see the direction of the trend more clearly.

The core logic of the Moving Average strategy is just one sentence: **Use the direction, intersection, and arrangement of Moving Average to determine the trend, and then follow the trend. ** It does not predict tops and bottoms, it only follows the trend that has already formed.

## 2. Applicable scenarios and failure scenarios

- **Applicable to: unilateral trend market. ** When the price has a clear direction (continuously rising or falling), Moving Average can follow the trend well, filter out noise, and allow you to "stay in the trend."
- **Invalidation: Shocking market conditions. ** When the price goes back and forth in the range, Moving Average will ** frequently Golden Cross, Death Cross, and then Golden Cross ** - generating a large number of ** false signals **, allowing you to chase the rise, kill the fall, and repeatedly Stop Loss (echoing C2-5).

**In a word: Moving Average strategy is "the friend of the trend and the enemy of the shock". ** Before using it, first determine whether the current market is trending.

## 3. How to use (signals and rules)

Moving Average strategies commonly use three types of signals (all are **references, not buying and selling orders**):

1. **Golden Cross / Death Cross** (Double Moving Average):
   - **Golden Cross = The short-term Moving Average crosses the long-term Moving Average** from bottom to top, which is a bullish reference;
   - **Death Cross = The short-term Moving Average crosses the long-term Moving Average** from top to bottom, which is a bearish reference (the direction must not be reversed, see B2).
2. **Bull / Bearish Alignment** (Multiple Moving Average system, see B3):
   - **Bullish Alignment**: from top to bottom = price > short term > medium term > long term → strong rising structure, inclined to hold;
   - **Bearish Alignment**: From top to bottom = long term > medium term > short term > price → weak downward structure, tendency to avoid.
3. **Moving Average direction**: Moving Average is bullish upward, bearish downward, and moves flat.

In actual combat, "Golden Cross + Bullish Alignment" is often used as a structural reference for the long side, and "Death Cross + Bearish Alignment" is used as a short reference, and used in conjunction with Stop Loss.

## 4. Advantages, Disadvantages and Risks

**Advantages**: Simple and intuitive, widely known; can help you **seize the main rise/main decline period** and filter short-term noise; the rules are clear, easy to execute and backtest.

**Disadvantages and Risks**:

- **Lag**: Moving Average is the average of past prices. **When the cross signal appears, the price has often moved for a while** - you buy at Golden Cross and sell at Death Cross, it is destined not to be the lowest/highest point;
- **There are many false signals in shocks**: This is the biggest pain point of the Moving Average strategy (see above);
- **No guarantee of profit**: It is a trend reference. The trend may reverse at any time and may have a false breakthrough; **No Moving Average parameter can guarantee profit**.

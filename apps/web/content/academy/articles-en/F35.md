## 2. Run it completely in Midas: seven links

We use a schematic scenario to walk through the complete process of a Stroke transaction in the **full virtual** environment of Midas (each step corresponds to the method learned previously):

**Section 1: Analysis (continue with F30). **
Open Midas, use **K line + indicator (Moving Average / MACD / Bollinger Bands / RSI) + Chan Theory annotation + contract dimension**, **Hierarchical reading structure**: Is the current trend or shock? Where is the key position? Are there any opportunities that fit my strategy? (You can **refer to the AI decision card** for this step, but press F32 - compare it with your own judgment and make an independent decision.)

**Level 2: Planning (continue from F2). **
If you judge that there is an opportunity, write down the **trading plan**: reason for entry, entry point, Stop Loss level, Take Profit target, and position size. **Plan first, trade later. **

**Session 3: (Virtual) Entrance. **
As planned, enter the market in the **virtual trading** of Midas (again: the entry and exit points are **logical indications**, not buying and selling signals).

**Session 4: Risk/position management (continue from F4/F23). **
It has been decided before entering the market: the maximum loss of this Stroke** is 1%** of the total funds, and the position is reversed using **Position = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Loss Amplitude**. (If you are doing a **contract**: Stop Loss must be set before **Liquidation price** (E9), and use **low enough leverage** to ensure that Liquidation price is far higher than Stop Loss price (F23).)

**Section 5: (Virtual) Holding and Exiting (continue to F6). **
After entering the market, **hold and exit in strict accordance with the rules**: use the mobile Stop Loss to follow, and execute when the Stop Loss/target is reached - **Do not change the plan on the spot due to the floating profits and losses of the virtual account** (Do not carry orders, do not run around, do not withdraw Stop Loss).

**Session 6: Review (then F7). **
After the transaction is completed, use the four quadrants of **F7** to review: Is the process correct? Is it a mistake in judgment or execution? Are there any emotional disturbances? Summarize areas for improvement.

**Level 7: Backtest verification (continue from F18/F33). **
Take this set of rules to the **Strategy Research Laboratory for backtesting** to see its overall performance in history (net value, retracement, winning rate, profit-loss ratio), understand the characteristics, discover problems, and iterate.

# What is a Trading System: From Scattered Knowledge to a Complete System

> Midas Academy · Level 5 · Module 13 Trading System and Practical Combat

**Quick overview of this article**

- **Trading system = a complete and repeatable set of trading rules**, which is a "drawing" that organizes scattered tools.
- Scattered knowledge cannot be repeated, tested, and improved, and it does not mean it can be profitable; the system makes trading systematic and resistant to emotions.
- The complete system contains six parts: **analysis methods, entry and exit rules, risk management, fund/position management, psychological discipline, review**, and the cycle forms a **closed loop**.
- The core of the system is **repeatable, executable, and suitable for you**; ⚠️ It pursues stability and controllability, **not a guaranteed profit formula, no guarantee of profit, and no prediction**.

## 1. Concept introduction: Having tools does not mean you can build a house

After learning the first four levels, you already have a lot of tools in your hand: K-line, Moving Average, MACD, Bollinger Bands, RSI, Chan Theory, contract mechanism... But many people will find an embarrassing fact - after learning a lot of ** tools, when it comes to placing orders, they still rely on feeling and stick here and there. **

It's like having all the bricks, cement, and steel bars, but no drawings, so you can't build a stable house. **The trading system is the "drawing" that organizes all the tools. ** At the fifth level, we will integrate scattered knowledge into a complete system.

## 2. What is the trading system?

**The trading system is a complete set of repeatable trading rules. ** It does not answer an isolated question, but all the questions of a Stroke transaction from beginning to end:

- How do I judge the market situation? (Analysis method)
- What conditions must be met before I can enter the market, and how much should I enter? (Entry rules, positions)
- At what point would I **leave** the market - either with a profit or a loss? (Exit rules, Stop LossTake Profit)
- How can I **control risks** and ensure survival? (Risk and Money Management)
- How can I ensure that I actually follow these instructions? (Psychology and Discipline)
- How do I **improve** from each stroke? (Review)

Stringing these together into a logical and repeatable whole is a system.

## 3. Why a system is needed: scattered knowledge ≠ profitable

Trading without a system usually looks like this: buy based on MACD Golden Cross today, sell after hearing news tomorrow, and buy the bottom based on feeling the day after tomorrow... The basis for each decision is different. It cannot be repeated, tested, or improved. Profit and loss all depend on luck and emotion.

The value of the system lies in changing transactions from "feeling" to "systematization":

- **Repeatable**: The same situation is handled according to the same rules, and the results are statistically significant.
- **Testable**: Only when the rules are clear can you review whether they are effective or not.
- **Can be improved**: Only when you know which rule has a problem can you optimize it accordingly.
- **Anti-Emotions**: Rules set in advance can block your impulses at critical moments.

## 4. What parts does a complete system include?

A relatively complete trading system usually includes the following modules:

1. **Analysis method**: What do you use to judge the market (trend, structure, indicators, Chan Theory, etc.).
2. **Entry/Exit Rules**: What conditions are met to enter the market, where to stop loss, and where to take profit.
3. **Risk Management**: What is the maximum loss for a single Stroke and how to set Stop Loss.
4. **Fund/Position Management**: How much to invest in each Stroke and how to control the total position.
5. **Psychology and Discipline**: How to ensure that rules are followed and that no one changes his or her mind temporarily.
6. **Review mechanism**: How to record, review, summarize, and improve.

These six elements are indispensable - without analysis, you will not know what to do; without risk management, you will be out after a big loss; without discipline, no matter how good the rules are, they will not be implemented.

## 5. The system is a closed loop

These six modules are not stacked together side by side, but operate in a cyclical manner: analysis → entry according to the rules → management of risks and positions → exit according to the rules → review → use the review conclusions to optimize the rules → return to the analysis... This cycle goes on and on, and the system is polished to become more and more suitable for you in rounds of iterations.

## 6. The core of the system: repeatable, executable, and suitable for you

Finally, we must point out the three cores of the system and a red line:

- **Repeatable**: The rules are clear and consistent, not made up every time.
- **Executable**: You can really do it and bear it, instead of looking beautiful but unable to execute it.
- **Suitable for yourself**: Match your personality, time, funds and risk tolerance (F10 will talk about this specifically).

> ⚠️ It must be made clear: **The trading system is to make transactions "stable, repeatable, and risk controllable", not a "guaranteed profit formula". ** No system can guarantee profits or predict the future; what the system does is to implement "probability of winning" and "risk management" systematically, rather than eliminating losses and uncertainty. A good system pursues "long-term, overall long-term survival, and stable performance" rather than "every stroke is right."

# A Complete Chan Theory Walkthrough: Downtrend

> Midas Academy · Chan Theory Advanced · Complete Review

The previous article deduced the rising market. This article deduces **Down Market** - the process is exactly the same, except in the opposite direction, and the buying point is replaced by a selling point. Compare it with C1-14, and you will have a firmer grasp of Chan Theory's "up-and-down symmetry" structural logic.

> ⚠️Similarly: **Fiction for teaching purposes/indicates the falling market, does not name the real target, and does not make a judgment of "should sell or buy"**. The selling point is marked as **structural position** and is not a buy or sell order; when it comes to Go Short, you should pay more attention to the risks.

**Quick overview of this article**

- Falling market deduction and rising **complete mirror**: **K line includes → Fractal → Stroke → Segment (downward SegmentFeature Sequence orientation upward Stroke) → Central Hub (the value of ZG/ZD remains unchanged) → Trend Type (≥2 Central Hubs are sequentially lowered = downtrend) → selling point → terminal Divergence**.
- Sell point mirror buying point: **1 Sell = Divergence turning point in the upward trend, 2 Sell = Counterattack does not break the previous high, 3 Sell = Fall below Central Hub, counterattack does not fall back to Central Hub (does not break ZD)**.
- The terminal **bottom Divergence** (the green column area shrinks + the yellow and white line pulls back) indicates that the decline may be completed, but the **bottom Divergence ≠ immediately bottoms out** (will be passivated and has three evolutions).
- ⚠️ **Suggestions / Fictional market conditions, non-real targets, not trading recommendations, and no predictions**; Go Short / Leverage has amplified losses and Liquidation risks.

## 1. The first step: K line includes → Fractal → Connected to Stroke

Just like rising, start from the foundation:

1. **K line inclusion processing** (the downward direction is also subject to non-inclusion merging according to the rules);
2. **Find Fractal** (Top Fractal, Bottom Fractal);
3. **Connect Stroke** (at least 3 K lines between Fractal, no sharing).

In a falling market, you will see the Stroke alternating up and down in an **overall downward** background.

![C1-15 with picture 1](/academy-img/C1-15-配图1.png)

## 2. Step 2: Stroke → Segment

Connect Strokes into Segments (at least three Strokes, with overlap), and use **Feature Sequence** to determine the end of the Segment. Note: **Feature Sequence of the downward Segment, oriented upward Stroke** (opposite to the direction of the Segment, see C1-1) - do not reverse the direction.

## 3. Step 3: Find Central Hub

Find the overlapping area of at least three sub-level trends, draw the Central Hub, and keep the value ** unchanged **:

- **Central Hub upper edge ZG = the minimum value of three high points**;
- **Central Hub lower edge ZD = the maximum value of three lows**.

(The value has nothing to do with the direction. The algorithm of ZG/ZD in the falling market is exactly the same as that in the rising market.)

![C1-15 with picture 2](/academy-img/C1-15-配图2.png)

## 4. Step 4: Determine Trend Type

Count the Central Hub and look at the direction:

- There is only **one Central Hub** → consolidation;
- **Two or more Central Hubs that are decreasing in sequence** → **Downward trend** (Central Hubs are relaying down one level at a time).

## 5. Step 5: Mark the selling point (structural position, mirror buying point)

The target in the falling market is the **selling point**, which is a complete mirror image of the buying point (**full structural position, non-buying and selling orders**):

- **1 Sell**: The end of the upward trend before the decline and the turning point after the **trend Divergence** appears;
- **2 sell**: After 1 sell, the price reverses and then goes down, and the point where the price reverses and does not break the previous high**;
- **3 Sell**: After Central Hub was **downwardly broken**, the price reversed and did not return to the point where Central Hub (does not break ZD)**.

Note that the condition for selling 3 is "**not breaking ZD (lower edge of Central Hub)**" - and 3. buying "not breaking ZG" mirror, don't forget to mix it up.

![C1-15 with picture 3](/academy-img/C1-15-配图3.png)

## 6. Step 6: Use Divergence to see the "health" of the decline

Use **MACD** to see if there is a trend Divergence** at the end of the decline** - see the **green column area** when it falls:

- The price hits a new low, but the **green column area shrinks significantly** + the yellow and white lines pull back → **Bottom Divergence**, the decline is exhausted, and this decline may be completed;
-Similarly (continued from C1-18): **Bottom Divergence ≠ Immediately bottom out and rebound**—it may then reverse, may turn to consolidation, or may continue to hit new lows (passivation). Don't regard bottom diversity as a "guarantee for buying the bottom."

## 7. Review summary (including Go Short risk warning)

The deduction of the falling market is completely symmetrical to that of the rising market: the process is the same, the values are the same, and the selling point is the mirror image of the buying point. This reflects the "beauty of upper and lower symmetry" of Chan Theory's structure.

But one sentence must be added:

> ⚠️ If you go short based on this in the **contract**, please remember the risk of Go Short/Leverage - **amplifying profits will inevitably amplify losses and may liquidate**; and this article is only a **schematic demonstration of structural analysis**, **does not constitute Go Short advice, and does not predict whether the price will continue to fall**. Divergence at the bottom may also be passivated and the decline may continue.

# Breakout Strategy

> Midas Academy · C3 Strategy Topic Library

"False breakthroughs" is the instinct of many people, but the biggest pitfall of breakthrough strategies - **false breakthroughs** - has also fooled countless people. This article explains the principles and practices of the breakthrough strategy, as well as how to deal with the unavoidable risk of false breakthroughs.

**Quick overview of this article**

- Breakthrough strategy = **After the price breaks through the key level, participate in the direction of the breakthrough** and want to seize the "starting point".
- **Applicable to post-consolidation breakthroughs/trend relay breakthroughs, and fails on the edge of shocks with frequent false breakthroughs**.
- Method: Find the key position → Wait for **effective breakthrough (closing firmly/increasing volume) and filter out false breakthroughs** → Enter the market with the trend → **Stop Loss is set on the other side of the breakthrough position**.
- ★**False breakthroughs are the biggest risk**; Chasing highs/chasing low cost differences; **Leverage amplifies the cost of false breakthroughs**; Profit is not guaranteed.
- ⚠️ Midas virtual disk can be tested/backtested (see the false breakthrough ratio and retracement), the AI ​​signal is for reference only; the signal is a reference, not an instruction, and not a prediction.

## 1. Strategy principles

The logic of the breakthrough strategy is: **When the price breaks through a key position (support/resistance, range boundary, previous high/previous low), it often means that the long-short balance is broken, and the price may continue to run in the direction of the breakthrough. ** (echoes F13)

Think of it as a "dam": the price is "blocked" by the upper and lower boundaries in the range; once the "dam" on a certain side is broken, the pent-up power is released, and the price may accelerate in that direction. The breakthrough strategy is to seize this "starting point".

## 2. Applicable scenarios and failure scenarios

- **Applicable to: breakthrough after consolidation and breakthrough in trend relay. ** If the price trades sideways for a long time and then breaks through, or if the price continues to break through the previous high/low during the trend, the "gold content" of the breakthrough is higher.
- **Expiration: An environment with frequent false breakthroughs, especially at the edge of a volatile market. ** The price breaks through the boundary a little bit and then **retracts** (false breakthrough), allowing those who are chasing the breakthrough to **catch at the highest/lowest price and then get trapped** (echoing C2-5).

**False breakthroughs are the inherent biggest enemy of breakthrough strategies. **

## 3. How to use (signals and rules)

1. **Find key positions**: support/resistance, upper and lower bounds of range, previous high and low points, Bollinger Bands track (C3-5), etc.;
2. **Wait for "effective breakthrough"**: It's not that the price touches the boundary to count as a breakthrough - you have to wait for **effective breakthrough** (such as **closing price** to stand firm at the breakthrough level, or **volume** to break through), and use these conditions to **filter false breakthroughs**;
3. **Enter the market in the direction of the breakthrough**;
4. **Stop Loss is set on the other side of the breakthrough level**: Once the price retracts within the breakthrough level (the false breakthrough is confirmed), stop loss and exit.

**"How to filter out false breakthroughs" and "How to quickly stop loss after false breakthroughs" are the two core skills of the breakthrough strategy. **

## 4. Advantages, Disadvantages and Risks

**Advantages**: Can **seize the starting point of the trend** (breakthroughs are often the starting point for market acceleration); follow the trend, have clear logic, and are easy to quantify.

**Disadvantages and Risks**:

- ★**Many false breakthroughs (the biggest pain point)**: Especially in volatile markets, "breakthroughs" are likely to be false, and repeated false breakthroughs will continue to wear out the Stop Loss;
- **Chasing high/chasing low risk**: The breakthrough point is often the high/low level of the stage, and the entry cost is not good;
- **Leverage magnifies the cost of false breakthroughs**: If you chase a breakthrough in the contract and are hit back by a false breakthrough, leverage will magnify the loss of each Stop Loss (echoing C2-5);
- **Profitability not guaranteed**.

# Before Chan Theory: Build the Right Mindset

> Midas Academy · Level 3 · Module 9 Introduction to Chan Theory

**Quick overview of this article**

- Chan Theory is an analytical tool, not a prediction artifact or the Holy Grail; it improves the quality of judgment, but does not eliminate uncertainty and does not guarantee profitability.
- The correct way to study is to understand the structure and logic, rather than memorizing formulas and looking for "must rise" signals; use it as a "language" to describe the trend.
- Be wary of the tendency to deify, metaphysically, and believe in Chan Theory, and to "use it to predict and hold heavy positions"; always cooperate with risk management.
- Chan Theory** has a threshold, you need to learn it step by step**, and learn step by step in the order of concepts.

## 1. Introduction of concepts: first correct your mentality, and then learn the methods

Before really getting into the concepts of Fractal, Stroke, and Central Hub, there is a "retreat" but extremely important content that must be talked about first - **The mentality of learning Chan Theory**.

Why put mentality before technology? Because in the process of spreading, Chan Theory has been given too much exaggeration and even metaphysical color. If you go to study with wrong expectations, you will not only fail to learn well, but you may also go astray and lose money. Correcting your mentality is the prerequisite for learning Chan Theory well.

## 2. Chan Theory is an analysis tool, not a prediction tool.

This is the myth that needs to be dispelled the most. Please remember one sentence: **Chan Theory is a set of analysis tools to help you "understand and describe the trend structure". It is not a prediction artifact, nor is it the "Holy Grail" that can make a sure profit. **

- It can help you break down the messy trends into Fractal, Stroke and Central Hub in a **structured manner**, giving you a clearer understanding of the current structure.
- But it **cannot tell you what the future will definitely be**. No analytical method, including Chan Theory, can eliminate market uncertainty.
- "Learning Chan Theory will enable you to accurately copy the bottom and escape the top / make stable profits" - this kind of statement is **wrong and dangerous**. If someone promotes this, be very vigilant.

The value of any analytical tool lies in **improving the quality of your judgment** rather than giving you a "necessarily correct" answer. Chan Theory is no exception.

## 3. Correct learning attitude: understand the structure rather than memorize formulas

Many people take a detour when learning Chan Theory because the method is wrong. Compare the two attitudes:

**❌ Wrong learning method (be careful): **
- Memorize Chan Theory's conclusions as "mantras" without understanding the logic behind them.
- I want to find the "must rise", "must fall" and "accurate buying and selling points" signals from Chan Theory.
- Turn Chan Theory into something metaphysical and mystical, and the more you learn, the more it becomes divorced from market reality.

**✅ Correct learning method: **
- **Understand the structure and logic of each concept**: Why is Fractal so defined, why is Stroke so connected, and what does Central Hub mean.
- Treat Chan Theory as a set of "language" to describe trends, rather than a set of "fortune-telling formulas".
- Always be aware of its **limitations**: it describes structures that have already happened and are happening now, not a promise for the future.
- Everything you learn must be used in conjunction with risk management.

## 4. Be wary of "going crazy"

The original text of Chan Theory is obscure and has many interpretations. Various schools have formed around it in the market, many of which are overly exaggerated and even metaphysical interpretations. There are several tendencies to be especially wary of when studying:

- **Regard Chan Theory as belief**: Believe that it can explain and predict everything, and will not listen to any doubts.
- **Overcomplicating**: Digging into the minutiae, but forgetting that the fundamentals of trading are risk and probability.
- **Use it to "predict" and make heavy bets**: Treat "analyzed possibilities" as "determined future" and ignore risk management.

A healthy attitude is: **use Chan Theory as one of many analytical tools**, learn it rationally, use it critically, and always remain in awe of the market.

## 5. Step by step and accept the threshold

One final point: Chan Theory does have a **learning threshold**. Its concepts are intertwined - if you don't understand the relationship between K lines, it will be difficult to understand Fractal; if you don't understand Fractal, you won't be able to draw Stroke well; if you don't understand Stroke and Segment, you won't be able to understand Central Hub.

So please accept the fact that it needs to be done step by step, and work through one concept step by step, without skipping a step or rushing for success. In the following articles, we will start from the most basic "K-line inclusion relationship" and build up step by step.

# Left-Side Trading vs. Right-Side Trading

> Midas Academy · Level 5 · Module 16 Trading Strategy Types

**Quick overview of this article**

- **Trading on the left** = Make arrangements in advance** before the turning point is confirmed** (going against the trend, buying the bottom and finding the top); **Trading on the right** = Entering after** the turning point is confirmed (following the trend, following the trend).
- Trade-off: **The cost on the left side is better but it is easier to "catch a flying knife"**; **The right side is more certain but gives up the starting profit and may encounter false confirmations**.
- ⚠️ **The risk of "buying the bottom" on the left is extremely high in the decline**; **There is no way to accurately buy the bottom/escape to the top** (the top and bottom are only known after the fact); do not predict the turning point.
- There is no absolute advantage or disadvantage between the left and right sides, it is a choice (echoing "suitable for you"), but the right side is usually safer for novices; both left and right sides must be combined with Stop Loss**.
- ⚠️ Red line: cannot accurately copy the bottom and escape the top, does not predict the turning point, goes against the trend + leverage is especially dangerous; **No guarantee of profit, risk management is required**, what worked in the past does not mean it will work in the future.

## 1. Concept introduction: Is it "ambushing in advance" or "waiting for confirmation before proceeding"?

F11 When talking about classification dimensions, we mentioned the **left/right** of "according to entry timing". In this article, we will single out this dimension and explain it thoroughly.

Imagine the same market trend of "first falling, then rising, and bottom reversal". The two types of traders have completely different approaches:

- A type of people start buying when the price is still falling and the bottom has not yet appeared - they feel that "it is almost the bottom" and they ambush in advance. This is the **left**.
- Another type of people wait until the price really turns upward and the trend is confirmed before entering the market - they want to "see the evidence" before acting. This is the **right side**.

Which one is better? Behind this is a trade-off between two entry philosophies. The pain points are also very real: people on the left often "buy the lows halfway up the mountain", while people on the right often "wait for confirmation and then chase the highs". This article will help you clarify it.

## 2. What are left-hand transactions and right-hand transactions?

Taking an **inflection point** (such as a bottom) as the "central axis", it depends on whether you enter the market to the left or right of it:

- **Left side trading**: Arrange in advance before the inflection point** is confirmed** (**left side** of the inflection point on the graph).
  - The essence is **countertrend/prejudgment**: the price is still falling, and you expect it to bottom out soon, buy in advance (**hunt for the bottom**); or the price is still rising, and you expect it to peak soon, sell in advance/Go Short (**hunt for the top**).
  - Keywords: ** advance, predict, go against the trend, buy the bottom and find the top. **

- **Right Side Trading**: Enter the market after** the turning point** is confirmed (**right** of the turning point).
  - The essence is **Following the trend/Following**: wait until the price really **turns upward, and the trend or breakthrough is confirmed**, then buy and follow.
  - Keywords: **wait, confirm, follow the trend, follow. **

One sentence distinction: **The left side is "bet on a turning point that has not happened yet", and the right side is "wait for the turning point to happen before following up". **

## 3. Their respective advantages and disadvantages

The left and right sides are a typical set of **risk and return trade-offs**, each with its own advantages and disadvantages:

**Transactions on the left:**

- ✅ **Advantage: better cost. ** If you copy the right bottom (or pick the right top), your entry price will be closer to the real turning point, and ** the potential profit margin will be larger **.
- ❌ **Disadvantage: Easy to "catch flying knives". ** You thought it had bottomed out, but in the end the price only dropped to "halfway up the mountain" and ** continued to fall, getting deeper and deeper**. Because the turning point cannot be accurately predicted, the left side is essentially betting on a turning point that has not yet happened, and may not happen at all as you expect.

**Transaction on the right:**

- ✅ **Advantage: Higher certainty. ** You enter the market only after the trend/breakthrough is confirmed. If you stand on the side of "the direction that has already happened", you have a relatively higher chance of winning and avoid "catching a flying knife".
- ❌ **Disadvantages: Give up the initial profit, and may encounter false confirmations. **Waiting for confirmation means that you **buy more expensive** (giving up the profit from the turning point to the confirmation point); and the confirmation may also be "fake" (false breakthrough, see F13), leading to being deceived into entering the market.

## 4. ★Key cognition: the risk of buying the bottom and "the inability to accurately buy the bottom and escape the top"

This section is the core red line of this article and must be explained clearly:

**1. The "bottom buying" on the left side is in a decline, and the risk is extremely high. **
"Catching flying knives" is one of the most typical ways for novices to lose a lot of money. A decline that has not really bottomed out can be far deeper and longer than you think. You thought you were at the bottom, but in the end you were just at a certain position in the decline relay, and there is still a long way to go. Repeatedly buying the bottom in a strong downward trend is the reason why many people's accounts shrink rapidly.

**2. There is no way to accurately copy to the lowest level and escape to the highest level. **
Please completely give up the illusion of "accurately buying the bottom to escape the top": **The real top and bottom can only be confirmed by looking back afterwards** - at the moment, you never know whether this is the lowest point. Any method or person who claims to be able to "accurately copy the bottom and escape from the top" cannot be trusted. What we can do is to act based on probability in a certain area near the turning point instead of "grabbing that precise point".

![F16 with picture 3](/academy-img/F16-配图3.png)

**3. There is no absolute advantage or disadvantage between the left and right sides, it is a choice, but the right side is usually safer for novices. **
-Whether you choose the left side or the right side depends on your judgment, risk tolerance and personality (echoing F10 "Suitable for You").
- But generally speaking, **for novices, the right side (following the trend, waiting for confirmation) is safer than the left side (countertrend, buying the bottom)** - because standing on the right side in the confirmed direction, it is not easy to catch the flying knife. The left side requires strong judgment, discipline and risk tolerance, and the threshold is higher.

**4. No matter the left or right side, it is not a "prediction" and must be combined with Stop Loss. **
They are two different ways of coping, not methods of "predicting the turning point". Even on the left side, it shouldn't be "take a gamble and hold on to it" - there should be a clear Stop Loss (if it falls below a certain position, admit that you made a mistake and leave the market). **Botting on the left without Stop Loss is the most dangerous way to play. **

## 5. Applicability and risks

- **Left side**: Suitable for mature traders with strong judgment, high risk tolerance, and strict Stop Loss; ⚠️ The risk of buying the bottom in a downward trend is extremely high, and it is the hardest hit area for "catching a flying knife". Novices should be extra cautious.
- **Right side**: Relatively stable and highly certain, suitable for most people (especially novices); the price is lower cost and the possibility of false breakthroughs.
- ⚠️ **Pay special attention to** under leverage: Use leverage to buy bottoms on the left. Once the "flying knife" continues to fall, the loss will be **quickly amplified, or even liquidation** - going against the trend + leverage is a dangerous combination.

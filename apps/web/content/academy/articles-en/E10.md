## 1. Concept introduction: use "zero real risk" to understand "high-risk" contracts

At this stage, we have repeatedly emphasized that contracts are high-risk instruments, and liquidation, leverage, and emotional risks are intertwined. Is there a way that allows you to deeply understand the operation of the contract market without having to bear real financial risks?

This is exactly the positioning value of Midas - **Midas is a fully virtual analysis terminal that allows you to understand, analyze, and practice high-risk contract markets in a zero-real risk environment. **

## 2. Midas encryption: mainly Perpetual Contract

Midas' encrypted channel uses Perpetual Contract as the main data source and analysis object, and spot as a supplement. The reason has been mentioned before (A11, each article in this level): Perpetual Contract can provide richer "emotion and structure" data, which is suitable for in-depth analysis.

Around Perpetual Contract, Midas provides multiple analysis dimensions to map the concepts learned at this level.

## 3. Contract analysis dimensions provided by Midas

Match the knowledge points in the previous articles with the functions of Midas:

- **K-line chart + technical indicators**: Overlay Moving Average, MACD, Bollinger Bands, RSI, etc. (corresponding to Group B) to observe price and momentum.
- **Chan Theory Marking**: Automatically mark Fractal, Stroke, Central Hub, and buying and selling points (corresponding to Group C) to understand the trend in a structured way.
- **Contract Dimension Chart**: Displays contract market-specific data such as **Position OI** (E5), **Long-Short Ratio** (E6), **Funding Rate** (E2), etc.
- **AI Decision Card/AI Sandbox Diagnosis**: AI provides long and short judgments, scores, confidence levels, and analysis of long and short structures.

Together, these dimensions help you gain a three-dimensional understanding of the current status of the contract market from multiple perspectives such as price, momentum, structure, and sentiment.

## 4. The value of full virtualization: "pay tuition" in a safe place

Many of the risks of contracts require "personal experience" to fully understand them - but the cost of experiencing them with real money is too high. Midas' **Full Virtual Transaction** just solves this contradiction:

- **All transactions use virtual funds and do not involve the sale or purchase of any real assets. ** You can fully experience the entire process of opening a position, holding a position, setting up Stop Loss, and even "Liquidation", and the losses are only virtual numbers.
- **Leave the "mistakes you should make" in the virtual environment**: Learn here why high leverage is dangerous, how Liquidation occurs, and how emotions affect decision-making - these lessons have zero real cost.
- Cooperate with the closed loop (A12) of "Learn → Practice → Review" to establish a solid understanding of the contract and risk awareness.

**Understand a high-risk market with zero real risk** - This is the greatest value of the virtual environment.

#How to use Chan Theory and how not to be misled by it

> Midas Academy · Level 3 · Module 11 Chan TheoryCentral Hub and Buying and Selling Points

**Quick overview of this article**

- The **reasonable usage** of Chan Theory: structured understanding of trends and assistance in judging the current position; it is a "mirror to see the structure clearly", not a "crystal ball to predict the future".
- The **limitations** of Chan Theory: ambiguity (the division is subjective), hysteresis (based on the trend that has occurred), and the need to be combined with other analyses.
- **Common Misunderstandings**: Mechanical application of buying and selling points, excessive pursuit of accuracy, deification and metaphysics - be careful.
- Chan Theory is only a part of the **complete trading system** and must be used in conjunction with risk management** with rationality and restraint.

## 1. Concept introduction: After learning, you must be able to "use it with restraint"

At this point, you already know the core concepts of Chan Theory’s special topics—including relationships, Fractal, Stroke, Segment, Central Hub, and buying and selling points. But learning a concept and using it correctly are two different things.

Chan Theory is a powerful analytical framework, but it is also the easiest to be biased and deified. This article is the end of the Chan Theory special topic, specifically talking about: **How ​​to use Chan Theory reasonably and how to avoid being misled by it. **

## 2. Reasonable usage of Chan Theory

To make good use of Chan Theory, the key is to understand what it can do:

- **Structured understanding of trends**: It can dismantle a complex price period into Fractal, Stroke, Segment, and Central Hub, allowing you to see the "skeleton" of the trend clearly, instead of being led by the superficial rise and fall.
- **Auxiliary judgment of current position**: Through the structure, you can roughly judge "it may be in a Central Hub shock", "this is a callback of a rising segment", etc. - that is, you know where you stand in the structure**.

In a word: The value of **Chan Theory is to help you "see the structure clearly and locate the present moment", so as to make your judgment more based. ** It is a "mirror" to help you see the trend clearly, not a "crystal ball" to predict the future.

## 3. Limitations of Chan Theory: ambiguity, lag, need to be combined with other

No matter how powerful a tool is, it has its limits. Chan Theory should pay special attention to three limitations:

1. **Ambiguity (subjectivity)**: This is the most realistic limitation of Chan Theory. For the same trend, **different people may divide it into different Stroke, Segment, and Central Hub** - especially when the market is in progress and the right side has not yet finished, there are often multiple possibilities for division. Chan Theory is not as uniquely certain as "1+1=2". Its application has a subjective judgment component.
2. **Hysteresis**: All structures of Chan Theory are based on prices that have **already occurred**. Fractal needs three K lines to confirm, and Stroke and Segment need to be completed before it can be determined - this means that the confirmation of the structure naturally **lags**, and it is clear after the fact, but often blurred during the event.
3. **Need to be combined with other analyses**: Chan Theory describes the geometric structure of prices, and it **does not include** fundamentals, funding, market sentiment and other information. Relying on Chan Theory alone, without looking at other things, is an incomplete perspective.

## 4. Common misunderstandings: Be wary of these usages

Based on the reminders in the previous articles, let’s list the most common misunderstandings. Please be sure to avoid:

- **Use buying and selling points as operating instructions mechanically**: Buy when you see a "buy point" and sell when you see a "sell point". Ignoring it is just a structural description and will fail.
- **Excessive pursuit of accuracy**: Obsessed with "accurate to a certain point" and "calculated to a certain minute", treating things that are subjective and probabilistic in nature as precision instruments.
- **Deification and metaphysics**: Regard Chan Theory as a "bible" that can explain and predict everything, and refuse to listen to doubts - this is the most dangerous way to learn Chan Theory.

## 5. Put Chan Theory into a complete trading system

Finally, and most importantly - **Chan Theory is only one component of the complete trading system, not the whole. ** A relatively complete system includes at least:

- **Risk Management**: Stop Loss, position control - this is the bottom line that cannot be replaced by any analysis method.
- **Other analysis dimensions**: Mutual confirmation of other technical tools, fundamentals, market environment, etc.
- **Discipline and Mentality**: No matter how good the analysis is, it is useless without execution discipline.
- **Review and Iteration**: Continuously review and revise your judgment.

**No matter how sophisticated Chan Theory is, it must be used in conjunction with risk management. ** Treat it as one tool among many and use it rationally so that you can benefit from it instead of being misled by it.

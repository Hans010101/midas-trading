# Differences Between Crypto, US Stocks, A-Shares, and HK Stocks

> Midas Academy · Phase 1 · Module 3: Understanding the Four Major Markets

## I. Concept Introduction: Same "Trading," Very Different Rules

Crypto, US stocks, A-shares, and HK stocks are the four most commonly encountered markets for many people, and they are exactly the four markets covered by Midas. They all follow the basic logic that "price is determined by supply and demand," but there are significant differences in **trading hours, trading systems, ability to Short, and volatility characteristics**.

Failing to understand these differences can easily lead to pitfalls—for example, getting used to "buying and selling on the same day" in A-shares, only to lose out in other markets or vice versa due to different rules. This article objectively outlines the main rule differences between the four, **stating rules only, without judging which market is better**.

> ⚠️ Note: Specific rules for each market (trading sessions, limit-up/limit-down percentages, shorting barriers, settlement cycles, etc.) are set by their respective exchanges and regulatory bodies and **may change with policy adjustments**. This article is an overview to help understand market differences; please refer to the official latest rules before actual trading.

## II. Crypto Market: 7×24 Hours, High Volatility, Bidirectional

The most distinct features of the crypto market (referring mainly to mainstream crypto assets) are:

- **24-Hour Continuous Trading**: Trading 7 days a week, 24 hours a day, with no market closure. Severe volatility can occur at any time of the day.
- **Generally Higher Volatility**: Crypto asset prices often fluctuate more violently compared to traditional stock markets.
- **No Limit-Up/Limit-Down Limits**: There is no fixed percentage ceiling for single-day gains or losses; theoretically, prices can swing significantly in a short period.
- **Can Long or Short**: Through derivatives like Perpetual contracts, you can Long and also Short conveniently (Spot generally only allows Long).
- **Flexible Trading (T+0)**: You can sell immediately after buying; there are no holding time restrictions.

These characteristics mean **both opportunities and risks are amplified**—especially when combined with contract leverage, risk is significantly higher (see related articles on Leverage and Contracts).

## III. US Stock Market: Concentrated Hours, Same-Day Trading, Circuit Breakers

Main rules for US stocks (US stock market):

- **Trading Hours**: Regular trading hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30–16:00 Eastern Time (approx. 6.5 hours), plus Pre-market and After-hours sessions (liquidity is usually lower). Note to convert to your local time zone.
- **Same-Day Trading (T+0)**: Stocks bought can be sold on the same day. (Supplement: The US previously had the **"Pattern Day Trader" (PDT) rule** for small margin accounts—accounts with less than $25,000 were limited in frequent same-day trading. This rule **was cancelled in June 2026** and replaced with a risk-based **intraday margin framework**. However, margin trading itself still has barriers and rules; please refer to your broker's specific regulations.)
- **No Fixed Daily Limit-Up/Limit-Down**, but **Circuit Breakers / Volatility Halt Mechanisms** are set: When an individual stock or the entire market experiences extreme volatility, temporary halts are triggered to "cool down."
- **Can Short**: Achieved by borrowing stocks to sell short through a margin account, subject to relevant rules.

## IV. A-Share Market: T+1, Limit-Up/Limit-Down, High Shorting Barriers

The rules of A-shares (Mainland China stock market) differ most obviously from the above two, with three landmark features:

- **T+1 Trading**: **Stocks bought on the same day can only be sold on the next trading day**. This is the most important point for A-share beginners—different from the "sellable on the same day" rule in Crypto, US stocks, and HK stocks.
- **Limit-Up/Limit-Down Limits**: Single-day price gains and losses have a fixed upper limit. The Main Board is typically **±10%**; ChiNext and STAR Market are **±20%**; Special Treatment (ST) stocks on the Main Board have been **uniformly raised from the original ±5% to ±10% (effective from July 2026)**, while ST stocks on ChiNext and STAR Market are **±20%** (with special rules for the initial listing period of new stocks). The existence of limit-up/limit-down limits restricts the single-day fluctuation range of individual stocks.
- **High Shorting Barriers**: Ordinary investors shorting (margin selling) are subject to many restrictions. They need to meet the qualification requirements for margin trading (such as certain asset thresholds and trading experience), and the list of shortable securities is limited. Therefore, A-shares are **primarily Long-focused**.
- **Trading Hours**: Monday to Friday, 9:30–11:30 AM and 13:00–15:00 PM (Beijing Time).

## V. HK Stock Market: Same-Day Trading, No Fixed Limit-Up/Limit-Down

Main features of HK stocks (Hong Kong stock market):

- **Trading Hours**: Monday to Friday, 9:30–12:00 AM and 13:00–16:00 PM (Hong Kong Time).
- **Same-Day Trading (T+0)**: Stocks bought can be sold on the same day, with no T+1 restrictions like A-shares.
- **No Fixed Daily Limit-Up/Limit-Down**: Unlike A-shares, there is no hard percentage ceiling for gains or losses. However, some large securities have a **"Volatility Control Mechanism (VCM)"**, which triggers a brief cooling-off period when prices fluctuate violently in a short time.
- **Can Short**: Shorting is allowed for **designated securities** (within the list of securities eligible for shorting set by the exchange).

## VI. Horizontal Comparison: A Table to Understand Key Differences

Comparing the key rules of the four major markets together (overview, subject to official latest rules):

| Dimension | Crypto | US Stocks | A-Shares | HK Stocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trading Hours | 7×24 Hours | Mon-Fri approx. 6.5 hours (+ Pre/Post) | Mon-Fri 9:30–11:30, 13:00–15:00 | Mon-Fri 9:30–12:00, 13:00–16:00 |
| Trading Cycle | T+0 (Buy/Sell anytime) | T+0 (Original PDT small account limits cancelled in June 2026) | **T+1** (Buy today, sell next day) | T+0 (Can buy/sell same day) |

| Volatility Characteristics | Generally Higher | Medium | Medium (single-day constrained by limit-up/limit-down) | Medium |

## VII. Key Takeaways

- The basic logic of the four markets is the same, but differences in **trading hours, trading cycles, limit-up/limit-down, shorting, and volatility** are significant.
- **Crypto**: 7×24 hours, high volatility, no limit-up/limit-down, bidirectional contracts, T+0.
- **US Stocks**: Regular hours approx. 6.5 hours, T+0 (original PDT restrictions for small accounts cancelled in June 2026), no fixed limit-up/limit-down but has circuit breakers, can Short.
- **A-Shares**: **T+1**, has limit-up/limit-down (Main Board ±10% etc.), high shorting barriers, primarily Long-focused.
- **HK Stocks**: T+0, no fixed limit-up/limit-down (some have VCM), can Short designated securities.
- Rules may change with policy adjustments; please refer to **official latest rules** before actual trading.

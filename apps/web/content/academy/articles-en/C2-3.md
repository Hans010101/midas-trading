# How Funding Rates Erode Long-term Positions

> Midas Academy · C2 Contract Case Library (Virtual Suggestion Review)

Many people only focus on the rise and fall of prices when making contracts, but forget that Perpetual Contract also has a continuously deducted cost - Funding Rate**. This article uses a schematic case to see how a "long-term holding" contract position can be "gnawed away" by the Funding Rate bit by bit.

> ⚠️ The whole process is virtual/suggestive, the numbers are all assumptions, no real targets are named, no real rates are predicted, and no recommendations are constituted.

## 1. Scene setting (align the mechanism first)

First reiterate the mechanism of Group E (the direction cannot be reversed):

- **Funding Rate** is a mechanism unique to Perpetual Contract, which is collected and paid regularly between **long and short parties (usually settled every few hours, **not a handling fee** paid to the platform);
- **Positive rate: longs pay to shorts; negative rate: shorts pay to longs. **

Setting the scene (made-up numbers):

- The market is in a **positive rate** for a long time (that is, **people who go long will continue to pay people who go short**);
- Trader E is optimistic about the market outlook, **Open Long and intends to hold it for a long time**;
- His ** margin is 1,000 yuan, leverage is 10 times, nominal exposure = 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 yuan**;
- Assume that the average **Funding Rate during this period is about 0.03%/day** (simplified as calculated on a daily basis; in fact, it is settled on a periodic basis, and the rate will fluctuate).

## 2. Operation process

E's idea was simple: "I'm optimistic about it, hold on, and wait for it to rise." So he left it alone after Open Long and held it for 90 days.

But he ignored one thing: **Funding fees are calculated based on "nominal exposure" and are deducted every day. **

- **Daily funding fee = exposure 10,000 × 0.03% = 3 yuan**;
- After holding for **90 days**, the accumulated capital fee = 3 × 90 = **about 270 yuan**;
- And his margin is only **1,000 yuan** - **270 yuan, which is equivalent to 27% of the principal (margin)! **

In other words: **Even if the price remains unchanged and does not increase by a penny during these 90 days**, E has **quietly lost about 27% of its principal** - all eaten up by funding fees. If the price increases only slightly, it may not be enough to cover the capital fee.

## 3. Dismantling of key nodes

**Node 1: Funding fees are calculated based on "exposure", not "margin". **
This is the most easily overlooked point. E's margin is only 1,000, but the funding fee is calculated based on an exposure of **10,000** - **Leverage magnifies not only the profit and loss, but also the base of the funding fee**. 10 times leverage means that your capital fee is also paid at 10 times the position.

**Node 2: The longer the time, the more severe the erosion. **
Funding fees are deducted every cycle, like "rent for holding positions". Short-term entry and exit may not be deducted a few times and have little impact; but if you hold it for a long time, it will accumulate over time. The longer you hold it, the more substantial the Stroke "rent" will be.

**Node 3: Under the positive rate, long-term Go Long is a "position against the wind". **
In the long-term **Go Long** in the **positive rate** market, it means **paying money out every day** (longs pay shorts). Even if you are in the right direction, your "profit" must first be deducted from the capital fees paid along the way before you can truly get it. E's fault is not to be bullish, but to hold the position for a long time without including the funding fee.

**Node 4: The spot does not have this Stroke cost. **
If E is held for a long time in **spot** (buy and hold currency), there is **no funding fee** (spot defaults to no leverage and no funding fee). This is also an important difference between contracts and spot - the "carrying cost" of contracts is higher than that of spot. For long-term "settled" demand, sometimes spot goods are more worry-free.

## 4. Key points of review

- **If you hold the Perpetual Contract for a long time, you must include the capital fee in your account**: It is a "position rent" that is continuously deducted, calculated based on the exposure and deducted based on the period. The long-term accumulation is considerable (90 days in this example ≈ 27% of the margin);
- **Look in the right direction ≠ Net profit**: The profit must be deducted from the capital fees paid along the way before you can truly get it; long-term Go Long under a positive rate is a "headwind";
- **The base of the funding fee is the exposure, not the margin**: Leverage magnifies it - this is one of the reasons why "high leverage long-term holding" is particularly uneconomical;
- **See clearly the direction of the fee rate**: Long positions with positive fee rates pay short positions, short positions with negative fee rates pay long positions (don’t remember it backwards); before holding a long-term position, first see clearly whether you are a "payer" or a "charging party";
- **For long-term "tun" demand, spot may be more economical**: There is no capital fee for spot, and there is no such layer of continuous erosion.

One sentence to end: The cost of a contract is not only the moment you open or close a position, but also every day you "hold it". ** Before holding a contract for a long time, first calculate the "invisible rent" of Stroke.

## 5. Main points of this article

- **Funding Rate** is a mechanism that is unique to sustainability and is regularly collected and paid in multiple spaces: **longs with positive rates pay shorts, and shorts with negative rates pay longs** (the direction cannot be reversed).
- Illustrative case: exposure 10,000, about 0.03%/day, holding for 90 days, accumulated ≈ **270 yuan**, equivalent to **27%** of the 1,000 margin - the price does not move and also erodes about 27% of the principal.
- Key: Funding fees are calculated based on exposure (not margin), leverage amplifies its base; **the longer the time, the more severe the erosion**; long-term Go Long under positive rates is a "headwind position"; **spot has no such cost**.
- ⚠️ The whole process is virtual/suggestive, numerical assumptions, the rates actually float and are settled on a periodic basis; the direction is correct ≠ net profit; it does not constitute suggestions or predictions.
